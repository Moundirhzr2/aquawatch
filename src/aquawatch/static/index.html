EXPLAINABLE BY DESIGN

Evidence before confidence.

Conservative rules, measurable results and explicit limitations.

Rule version 1.0.0

How the rules work

Sustained unusual consumption
Three consecutive daily values above both 3× the preceding 14-day median and that median + 250 L. At least 10 valid baseline intervals are required.
Counter moved backwards
A cumulative reading decreases. The interval is excluded from consumption; a reset, replacement or input error needs review.
Missing readings
At least one daily reading is absent between observations or before the explicit observation cutoff.
Billing mismatch
Recompute the invoice from its stated volume and tariff, with integer cents and half-up rounding. Differences greater than €0.01 are flagged.
Duplicate reading
A meter/date pair already exists. The incoming row is quarantined, including conflicting counter values.

What the benchmark means

Results below come from the fixed synthetic dataset, independently of your current investigation decisions or additional uploads.

Designed to reveal limitations

The fixture includes subtle 1.5× consumption events that the conservative rule can miss. A high score on synthetic data does not establish real-world performance.

Evaluation matches exact meter, exception type and event start date. The generator and detector live in separate modules; labels never enter the detector.

Invoice checks validate tariff arithmetic against stated volume. They do not verify the billed volume against meter records across resets, gaps or tariff changes.
